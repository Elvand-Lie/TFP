True Path

Your Data.
Your Choice.

This privacy policy explains how The Full Picture LLP handles information collected through True Path.

Answers and saved reports

Your answers and journey progress are stored in this browser tab so you can refresh or go back. Completed answers, scores and report content are saved on our server to create your report link. The record may include referral information and device category. Server report records expire after 90 days.

Report email and consent

Email is optional. When you request it, we collect your first name, email address and report consent to send the PDF and record the request. Marketing consent is a separate, optional choice. Requesting a report does not automatically give marketing consent.

Services that process the information

We use Vercel to host the site, Upstash to store report records, and Resend to deliver report emails. Report requests may be shared with The Full Picture team and, where configured, our customer relationship system to handle your request. Copies in email or that system may remain after the report record expires.

Sharing your report

Anyone with your report link can view its answers and results. The public report response excludes your email, first name and consent details. Keep your link private if you do not want others to see your results.

Usage events

True Path emits journey and link-click events with relevant scores, choices, referral information and device category for usage analysis. These events are available to the site's configured analytics integration.

Questions and requests

For questions about your data, access or deletion requests, or to withdraw marketing consent, contact The Full Picture. Include your report ID where available. Please avoid posting your private report link publicly.
